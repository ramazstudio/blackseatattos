Brandon Bear

Owner / Tattoo Artist

Brandon Bear is a seasoned, highly regarded tattoo craftsman based in Northern California, specializing in American Traditional tattooing. He is best known for his bold color palettes, solid black work, and distinctively crisp line quality.

Professional History & Background

With over 15 years of experience, Brandon has been active in the professional tattooing community since 2010. He built his way up from the bottom — starting as general shop help, moving into the shop manager role, and eventually completing a traditional apprenticeship under a veteran artist.

Artistic Style & Philosophy

His core aesthetic relies on classic imagery — like daggers, roses, eagles, and ships — executed with a personal, modern style twist. Brandon actively draws, paints, and prints extensive physical collections of custom tattoo "flash sheets" and travels across conventions to sell his high-quality prints.

He emphasizes a humble, traditional work ethic, focused around steady self-improvement, continuous painting, and providing an authentic, classic, and completely sterile tattoo shop environment.
