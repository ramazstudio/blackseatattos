Aftercare

Tattoo Aftercare

Proper care ensures a beautiful, long-lasting tattoo.

First 24 Hours

  • Leave the bandage on for the recommended time
  • Wash hands before touching your tattoo
  • Gently wash with lukewarm water & mild, fragrance-free soap
  • Pat dry with a clean towel — do not rub

During Healing

  • Apply a thin layer of recommended tattoo moisturizer
  • Keep the tattoo clean and dry
  • Wear loose clothing over the area
  • Drink plenty of water to help your skin heal

🚫 Avoid

  • 🚫 Swimming or soaking in water for at least 2–3 weeks
  • 🚫 Direct sunlight while the tattoo is healing
  • 🚫 Picking or scratching peeling skin
  • 🚫 Heavy workouts that cause excessive rubbing or sweating on the tattoo

Healing time: 2–4 weeks

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
